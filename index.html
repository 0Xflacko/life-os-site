⋆ Life OS

A personal operating system for one person.

Life OS is a private dashboard and set of background jobs that run on its owner's own computer. It organises work, content and day-to-day life in one place. It is a single-user tool and is not offered to the public.

What it does with Google

  • Reads the owner's Gmail to sort and summarise the inbox.
  • Creates reply drafts that the owner reviews. Nothing is sent automatically.
  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar to plan the day.

Details are in the privacy policy.
