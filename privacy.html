Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026

Life OS is a personal, single-user application run by its owner (github.com/0Xflacko) on the owner's own computer. The only Google account data it handles belongs to the owner, who signs in and grants access through Google's consent screen.

Data Life OS accesses

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): messages and metadata, to sort and summarise the inbox.
  • Gmail drafts (gmail.compose): to create reply drafts for the owner to review. Life OS does not send email automatically. Sending always takes the owner's own action.
  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly): events, to plan the owner's day.
  • Basic profile (openid, email): the account's email address, to tell the connected accounts apart.

How it is used

The data is used only to provide these features to the owner. Email and calendar content may be processed by the owner's own AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude, under the owner's personal subscription), only to produce summaries, priorities and drafts for the owner. Life OS does not sell data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone else.

Storage and security

Data and sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local database that only the owner can access. Nothing is uploaded to a Life OS server; there isn't one.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke Life OS's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the locally stored data at any time.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Life OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
